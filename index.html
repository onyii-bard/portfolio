You can call it curiosity.

I've always been fascinated by why people pay attention to some things and ignore others.

Why one piece of content gets shared thousands of times while another disappears unnoticed. Why certain ads feel impossible to scroll past. That curiosity is what pulled me into marketing.

Over the years, I've worked across content, SEO, email marketing, creator/B2B partnership, social media, and paid advertising, because I wanted to understand how each piece contributes to growth.

I grew up in Lagos, and that curiosity still drives everything I do.
